## Phase 1: Original 15 Mechanics
Completed 2026-04-10. Full report at /Users/alex/www/brainrot-games/research/game-mechanics-report.md

Top 5 recommended mechanics (by combined score):
1. Flappy Bird (15/15) -- trivial to build, max retry compulsion, proven ad model
2. Stack (14/15) -- simplest mechanic, high ad frequency, streak psychology
3. Snake (13/15) -- universal recognition, dead-simple implementation
4. Whack-a-Mole (13/15) -- ideal for meme character showcasing, pure reaction
5. Idle Clicker (12/15) -- highest daily engagement (5.3 sessions/day), monetization machine

## Phase 2: 30 New Mechanics Expansion
Completed 2026-04-10. Full report at /Users/alex/www/brainrot-games/research/30-new-mechanics.md

Top 10 new mechanics (by combined score out of 20):
1. Piano Tiles (18/20) -- trivial build, insane replay, ad after every death
2. Color Switch (17/20) -- 200M+ downloads, one-tap, Color Switch-proven model
3. Wordle Clone (17/20) -- built-in viral sharing (emoji grid), daily retention
4. Memory Match (16/20) -- ideal meme character showcase, universal recognition
5. Match-3 / Bejeweled (16/20) -- gold standard monetization, $1B+/yr genre
6. Hangman (15/20) -- zero physics, brainrot vocabulary potential
7. Merge/Evolution (15/20) -- highest ARPU genre, idle retention loop
8. Paper Toss (15/20) -- one gesture, perfect ad cadence
9. Basketball Shot (15/20) -- same physics as Paper Toss, different skin
10. Ball Bounce Timing (15/20) -- one tap, pure timing

**Why:** Studio now has 40 total mechanics cataloged (10 built + 30 new). This gives the roadmap enough variety for 6+ months of releases.

**How to apply:** Use Phase 1 top 5 for first wave of games. Use Phase 2 ranking to plan subsequent waves. Timing/Rhythm category (avg score 15.6/20) should be prioritized after initial 10 games are shipped. Word/Trivia category (avg 14.2) is underexplored and has highest viral potential for meme-themed content.
