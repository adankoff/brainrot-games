Brainrot Games is a casual HTML5 browser game company launching April 2026 with 2 games: Flappy Tralalero and Whack-a-Rot, themed around Italian brainrot memes, targeting ages 8-25.

**Why:** Bridging the resurgent HTML5 browser game ecosystem (Poki 625M annual players) with explosive demand for brainrot/meme content (Steal a Brainrot hit 25.4M CCU on Roblox).

**How to apply:** All financial analysis should account for near-zero cost structure (static hosting, AI-generated content), ad-supported primary revenue model, and the inherent uncertainty of meme lifecycle timing. Key risk: meme could peak within 6-12 months.

Revenue model at /Users/alex/www/brainrot-games/business/revenue-model.md covers three scenarios:
- Conservative (10K MAU): $873 year-1 cumulative
- Moderate (100K MAU): $12,741 year-1 cumulative
- Optimistic (500K MAU): $125,057 year-1 cumulative
- Probability-weighted expected value: ~$23,655 year-1
