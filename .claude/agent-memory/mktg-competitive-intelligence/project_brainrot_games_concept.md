Brainrot Games is a new company concept that will reskin classic casual game mechanics (flappy bird, idle clickers, survivors-like, etc.) with brainrot/internet culture themes (skibidi, Italian brainrot, sigma, etc.) and ship them as HTML5 browser games.

**Why:** The thesis is that browser gaming is resurging (Poki hit 625M annual players in 2025), brainrot culture is at peak mainstream relevance (Oxford Word of the Year 2024), and AI tools enable a tiny team to ship games at 2-4x historical pace.

**How to apply:** All competitive intelligence, market research, and strategy work should be framed around this specific niche — meme/internet-culture-themed HTML5 casual browser games targeting Gen Z and Gen Alpha audiences. The primary distribution channels are own website, Poki, CrazyGames, TikTok viral loops, and Reddit/HN for discovery.
