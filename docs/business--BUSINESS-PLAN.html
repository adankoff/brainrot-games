Brainrot Games — Executive Business Plan

April 2026

1. Company Overview

Name: Brainrot Games Mission: We make games that are bad for you in all the right ways. Tagline: your brain on memes.

Brainrot Games is a casual game studio that takes proven, addictive game mechanics (Flappy Bird, Whack-a-Mole, idle clickers, endless runners) and reskins them with trending internet culture — Italian brainrot characters, sigma/rizz vocabulary, Ohio memes, and whatever is currently melting Gen Alpha's brains on TikTok.

The games are free, browser-based HTML5 games. No download, no signup, no app store. Click a link, play immediately. Built to be shared.

2. Market Opportunity

Brainrot culture × casual gaming is a proven intersection at massive scale:

  • Steal a Brainrot on Roblox hit 25.4M concurrent users and generates ~$11M/month in revenue
  • "Brain rot" was Oxford Dictionary's 2024 Word of the Year
  • Browser gaming is resurging: Poki reached 625M annual players; the HTML5 games market is projected at $6B→$10.4B by 2035
  • Target demographic (ages 8-16): 83% play games weekly, 52% make in-game purchases, 6+ hours daily gaming time

The gap: Roblox is saturated with brainrot content. Mobile has mostly low-quality clones. The browser game space has massive demand (search traffic for "brainrot games" is growing) but no quality-first studio serving it.

TAM estimate: HTML5 games market: $6B (2026). Casual/hyper-casual mobile (which browser games compete with): $20B+. Our addressable slice: the intersection of meme-culture games × browser platform — estimated at $50-200M globally.

4. Competitive Advantage

  1. Speed of execution. Shared infrastructure means each new game is 1-5 days of incremental work. Competitors take weeks.
  2. Trend-native brand. The brand voice is authentically online, not a corporation cosplaying as a meme page.
  3. Zero-friction distribution. Browser-first means no app store gatekeeping, no download friction, no install conversion drop-off. A game is a URL.
  4. AI-accelerated production. 1-2 person team producing at the pace of a 5-person studio.
  5. No legacy baggage. No existing codebase, no team politics, no enterprise processes. Pure velocity.

5. Go-to-Market

Viral-first, social-native, zero-budget launch.

  • Primary channel: TikTok gameplay clips (15-second failures, score challenges, reaction bait)
  • Secondary channels: Reddit (r/webgames, r/InternetIsBeautiful), Discord, YouTube Shorts
  • Distribution platforms: Own website (full ad revenue) → Poki/CrazyGames (discovery) → itch.io (indie cred)
  • Influencer strategy: Micro-influencer outreach to gaming TikTokers (10K-100K followers). No budget — the pitch is "this is funny, try it."
  • Viral mechanics: Built into every game — shareable score cards, funny death screen messages, screenshot-worthy moments.

The growth loop: Player plays → dies hilariously → screenshots/records → shares to group chat/TikTok → friends click link → play immediately → repeat.

6. Revenue Model

Phase 1 (Launch): Ad-supported

  • Interstitial ads on death (every 3rd death)
  • Rewarded video for "continue" or score multiplier
  • Blended eCPM: $2-4 (web, mixed geo)

Phase 2 (Month 2+): Multi-channel

  • Poki/CrazyGames revenue share (50/50 on platform traffic)
  • Game licensing to portal networks ($200-2000 per deal)

Phase 3 (Month 3+): Diversified

  • Cosmetic IAP (character skins, $0.99-1.99)
  • Android app wrapper (Google Play)
  • Steam premium collection bundle ($2.99)

8. Risks & Mitigations

Risk Severity Mitigation
Meme theme decay — Italian brainrot fades High Mechanics stand alone; reskin with next trending meme in <1 day. Theme is the skin, not the skeleton.
AI game flooding — everyone can build these now High Compete on curation, brand, and cultural taste, not technical execution. Be the studio with good games, not just games.
TikTok regulatory risk — potential US restrictions Medium YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels as backup distribution. Reddit/Discord organic as base.
Low initial traffic — organic growth is slow High Expected. Budget 3 months before judging. Volume of games increases surface area.
Platform dependency — Poki/CrazyGames change terms Medium Own website is primary revenue source. Portals are supplementary.
Content appropriateness — parent/media pushback Medium Content is absurdist, not edgy. No violence, no sexual content, no bullying. Self-police aggressively.
